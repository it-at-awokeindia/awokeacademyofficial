About the Academy

Where skill meets purpose, and learners become professionals.

AWOKE Academy is the learning and professional development initiative of AWOKE India — a not-for-profit committed to awareness, awakening, and access. We build programs that turn ambition into capability: rigorous curriculum, real practice, and mentors who've done the work.

We serve students preparing for the world of work, professionals upskilling for the next chapter, women stepping into entrepreneurship, and organisations investing in their people.

Awareness

Bringing financial, digital and civic literacy to every learner.

Awakening

Building confidence, voice, and clarity of purpose alongside skill.

Access

Programs designed to reach small towns, colleges and rural cohorts.

Action

Placement, projects, and mentorship that turn learning into livelihood.

Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked.

Every AWOKE Academy program concludes with an industry-recognised certificate that includes the program name, duration, capstone project and assessment score. Featured programs (CBPP, Digital Marketing, Graphic Designing) carry additional endorsements from our partner network.
All three. Featured programs run in hybrid mode with weekly on-campus sessions. Short skill-development tracks are available fully online. Corporate and community programs are delivered on-site.
Fees vary by program length and format. Our team walks you through options — including scholarships for women learners, students from AWOKE India partner colleges, and community program participants — during the admission conversation.
Most short-format programs are open to any adult learner. Featured tracks like CBPP are open to graduates or final-year students. If you're unsure, book a counselling call — we'll help you find the right starting point.
All full-length programs include resume building, interview preparation, LinkedIn coaching, career counselling, and access to our placement partner network. Support continues after certification — as long as you need it.
